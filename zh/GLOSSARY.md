# 术语表 — Chinese game vocabulary

One word per idea, everywhere. Struct-type data (classes, weapons, abilities)
is in `runtime/terms.json`; this page is for everything else and agrees with it.

## Voice

- Simplified Chinese, mainland usage: 账号, 昵称, 设备, 点按, 长按.
- Players are strategy gamers, not crypto users. The chain stays out of sight:
  no 区块链, 钱包, 代币, 交易, 转账, 签名, Gas where a game word does the job.
- Narration, help text, system messages: clear and direct, 你, no 请 pile-ups.
- HR Bot, sign-up and corporate callers: 您, polite, a little stiff, corporate.
- Buttons, tabs, headers: two to four characters where possible.
- Full-width punctuation （，。！？：（）“”）; ellipsis is ……; a list joins with 、.
- A space between Chinese and Latin letters or digits (`最少 3 个字符`), none
  beside full-width punctuation.
- Numbers, units and ids stay as they are: `W`, `kW`, `PID`, `#`, `1-42`.
- Time left is `4天 23时 16分`.

Struct names are in `runtime/terms.json` (指挥舰, 战列舰, 星际战机, 护卫舰, …).
护卫 is also the word for a struct defending another; 防守方 is the player whose
planet is raided. Model names (Spearpoint, Rolling Thunder, …) and model numbers
stay in English.
